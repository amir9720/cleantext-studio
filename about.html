About CleanText Studio

Empowering digital creators, writers, and developers with instant, private text transformation utilities.

Our Mission

CleanText Studio was built to solve a simple yet widespread friction: cleaning up messy formatting, jagged PDF line breaks, intrusive tracking markers, and unorganized text data quickly and securely. We believe that basic data cleaning should be instantaneous, distraction-free, and accessible directly in your web browser.

Client-First Privacy

Unlike many online converters that send your documents or confidential notes to external cloud servers, CleanText Studio processes everything entirely on your local machine using modern client-side web technologies. Your text never leaves your device, guaranteeing total privacy and immediate speed.

Who We Serve

  • Writers & Editors: Seamlessly remove erratic line wraps from PDFs, fix casing formats, and strip unwanted markdown artifacts.
  • Developers & Data Analysts: Sanitize zero-width Unicode characters, convert raw CSV lines into Markdown tables, and escape JSON-safe strings.
  • Students & Researchers: Format references, organize messy text excerpts, and convert subtitle scripts into readable prose.
