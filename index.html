What is CleanText Studio?

CleanText Studio is a high-speed, client-side utility designed for copywriters, developers, data analysts, and students. It eliminates formatting quirks, sanitizes sensitive data, and converts non-standard text structures into production-ready formats without sending your sensitive information to any cloud servers.

Why Clean and Standardize Text?

  • PDF Formatting Artifacts: Copying paragraphs from PDF files often results in ragged, jagged line breaks at unpredictable margins. Our PDF cleaner restores uniform paragraph flow seamlessly.
  • Developer & Data Operations: Strip zero-width Unicode characters, escape complex multiline strings into JSON parameters, or convert raw CSV lists into GitHub-compatible Markdown tables instantly.
  • AI & LLM Sanitization: Easily remove asterisks, hash headers, and bold formatting symbols injected by chat assistants before pasting them into clean emails or formal documents.
  • Subtitles to Articles: Turn timestamped `.srt` or `.vtt` movie subtitle files into readable prose or book drafts in a single second.

Privacy & Security Guarantee

All operations are handled entirely through modern JavaScript inside your local web browser. Your inputs, documents, and credentials never cross the network or touch remote databases, making it compliant with strict confidentiality policies.
