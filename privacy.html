Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At CleanText Studio, the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by CleanText Studio and how it is used.

Client-Side Data Processing

All text inputs, data files, strings, logs, and information pasted into the utility forms on CleanText Studio are processed entirely on your local device (client-side) via your browser's JavaScript engine. None of your entered text is transmitted, stored, inspected, or saved on any remote servers or external databases.

Log Files and Analytics

Like many standard websites, CleanText Studio may collect non-personally identifiable log information commonly provided by web browsers and servers. This may include Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts to analyze trends and administer the site.

Cookies and Web Beacons

CleanText Studio uses standard cookies to store visitor preferences, record user-specific information on which pages the user accesses, and optimize the visitor experience.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on CleanText Studio.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to CleanText Studio and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Consent
